The problem

From 1 November, a Temu basket with 6 item types costs about 13,800 HUF extra.

Since 1 July 2026 every Temu order shipped from outside the EU pays customs duty for each different item type, and from 1 November a handling fee comes on top. Each item type adds about 2,300 HUF to your order, on top of the item prices. The more varied your basket, the more you pay.

How it works

Five steps, from sign-in to pickup.

The organizer places one real Temu order. You just add your cart and pick up your parcel.

  1. Sign up

    Set your area. Your exact address is never shown to anyone.

  2. Find cart friends nearby

    See nearby organizers, how many item types you share, how much they will front for you, and your estimated saving.

  3. Add your cart

    Share your Temu cart in one go, or add single items one by one.

  4. The organizer orders

    Everyone's items are merged into one checklist. The organizer places a single order, and each share is worked out from the real checkout.

  5. Meet, pay and pick up

    Collect your items at a public place or parcel locker, pay the organizer for your goods, and confirm with your pickup code.

We never handle money for goods. You pay the organizer in person at pickup, so nobody pays for anything they have not received.

Trust, safety and privacy

Pay only when you pick up. Meet in public. Your address stays private.

Pay at pickup

You pay the organizer only when you receive your items. Organizers may ask only for the cost of your goods, nothing extra.

Public handovers

Pickups happen at public places or parcel lockers, never at home. Missed pickups lead to strikes and suspension.

Your address stays private

Only your area is visible to others. The organizer's delivery address stays private too.

What else protects you

  • Proof of order: the organizer uploads the Temu order confirmation before the order is locked.
  • Private shopping list: visible only to the organizer of your order.
  • Your data, your call: delete your account at any time.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Something missing? Join the waitlist and we'll keep you posted before launch.

Is this a Temu service?

No. It is an independent service that helps people in the same area combine orders. It is not affiliated with Temu.

Who pays Temu?

The organizer places and pays one normal Temu order. You pay the organizer for your items when you pick them up.

What if my items do not arrive?

You do not pay for items you did not receive.

How do I know how much I'll save?

Before you join, you see how many item types you share with each nearby organizer and what you would pay in import charges with each of them.

Is this allowed?

Yes. Only real orders are combined, legally, and every order is declared normally.
