The problem

From 1 November, about 2,300 HUF in import charges for each different item type.

Since 1 July 2026 every Temu order shipped from outside the EU pays customs duty for each item type, and from 1 November a handling fee comes on top. The more varied your basket, the more you pay.

How it works

Five steps, from sign-in to pickup.

The organizer places one real Temu order. You just add your cart and pick up your parcel.

  1. Sign in with Google

    Set your district. No password, and your exact address is never shown to anyone.

  2. Find an order near you

    See nearby organizers, how many item types you share, how much they will front for you, and your estimated saving after fees.

  3. Add your cart

    Upload a screenshot of your Temu cart or paste product links. The app reads the items and you confirm them.

  4. The organizer orders

    The app merges everyone's items into one checklist. The organizer places a single order, and the app works out each share from the real checkout.

  5. Meet, pay and pick up

    Collect your items at a public place or parcel locker, pay the organizer for your goods, and confirm with your pickup code.

The app never handles money for goods. You pay the organizer in person at pickup, so nobody pays for anything they have not received.

Trust, safety and privacy

Pay only when you pick up. Meet in public. Your address stays private.

Pay at pickup

You pay the organizer only when you receive your items. Organizers may ask only for the cost of your goods, nothing extra.

Public handovers

Pickups happen at public places or parcel lockers, never at home. Missed pickups lead to strikes and suspension.

Your address stays private

Only your district is visible to others. The organizer's delivery address stays private and encrypted.

What else protects you

  • Proof of order: the organizer uploads the Temu order confirmation before the order is locked.
  • Private shopping list: visible only to the organizer of your order.
  • Data in the EU: AWS Frankfurt, deleted after a fixed period. Delete your account at any time.
  • Google sign-in: shares only your name and email with the app.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Something missing? Join the waitlist and we'll keep you posted before launch.

Is this a Temu service?

No. It is an independent app that helps neighbours combine orders. It is not affiliated with Temu.

Who pays Temu?

The organizer places and pays one normal Temu order. You pay the organizer for your items when you pick them up.

What if my items do not arrive?

You do not pay for items you did not receive.

Why the EUR 150 limit?

Above it Temu charges import fees differently and costs rise, so the app keeps each shared order under it (69,184 HUF of item prices at the current rate).

Is this allowed?

Yes. The app combines real orders legally, and every order is declared normally.
