3. Legal basis

We process your data based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in our emails or by emailing us. Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that happened before.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Unconfirmed sign-ups: deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Confirmed sign-ups: deleted automatically 12 months after confirmation at the latest, or earlier if you unsubscribe.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in automatic backups for up to 35 days, then it is gone for good.
  • Technical logs: if something goes wrong, the error message is kept for up to 30 days.

Automatic deletion happens within a few days after the deadline.

5. Who can access your data

We use a single provider, which only processes data on our instructions, under a contract with us:

  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL, 38 Avenue John F. Kennedy, L-1855 Luxembourg): hosting and delivering this website, storing the waitlist data and sending our emails. The site and the waitlist data are stored in AWS's Frankfurt (EU) data centre.
  • When you open the site, AWS's content delivery network (Amazon CloudFront) delivers the page to you and receives your IP address for technical reasons. We don't keep logs of visits.

6. Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, and we don't track visitors with analytics or advertising tools.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to

  • tell you what data we hold about you and give you a copy (Article 15 GDPR),
  • correct inaccurate data (Article 16),
  • delete your data (Article 17),
  • restrict processing (Article 18),
  • give you your data in a machine-readable format (Article 20).

We reply within one month at the latest. Email us at the address above.

If you think we have breached your rights, you can complain to the Hungarian data protection authority (Nemzeti Adatvédelmi és Információszabadság Hatóság, NAIH, Falk Miksa utca 9–11, 1055 Budapest, www.naih.hu, ugyfelszolgalat@naih.hu), to the authority in your EU country of residence, or go to court.

8. Changes

If this notice changes significantly, we'll let you know by email.
